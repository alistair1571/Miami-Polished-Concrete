Polished Concrete in Wynwood

Wynwood's galleries, restaurants, bars, breweries, shops and lofts mostly sit on old factory slabs from its decades as Miami's garment and manufacturing district. Polished concrete suits them perfectly, as long as the slab, the use and the schedule are handled right.

This page covers converted warehouse floors, the right finish for galleries, working around nightlife, brewery floors, new mid-rises, and when to schedule around Miami Art Week. It also covers the Design District and Midtown.

Converted warehouse and factory floors

Wynwood was one of the country's largest garment districts, alongside bottling, baking and light manufacturing, before artists began taking over its warehouses around the turn of the century. Most of those buildings still sit on their original industrial slabs.

  • Old oil and machine stains can be reduced by grinding but may not disappear. Many owners keep them as character.
  • Bolt holes and anchors from old machinery are cut down and filled.
  • Trenches and patches from past plumbing and equipment show as a different tone.
  • Layers of paint, from floors painted over the decades, have to be ground off, sometimes including mural overspray near walls.
  • Uneven slabs: grinding exposes more stone on high spots. A test panel shows the likely look.

If you want a uniform, clean look instead of industrial character, consider microcement or a coating.

Gallery floors

Polished concrete is the classic gallery floor: neutral, durable and easy to keep clean between openings. A few things matter more in a gallery than elsewhere:

  • Sheen. Matte or satin is usually best. High gloss reflects track lighting and can compete with the work on the walls.
  • Look. Cream or salt-and-pepper keeps the floor quiet; heavy exposed aggregate draws the eye.
  • Heavy loads. Crates, pallet jacks and lifts are fine on densified concrete, but repair spalls and joints first.
  • Events. Galleries rented for events see heavy foot traffic and spills; a guard and burnish help, and entrances benefit from matting.
  • Timing. Finish before artwork is reinstalled, and allow a day for the guard before heavy traffic.

Restaurants, bars and nightlife

Wynwood's restaurant and bar floors take spilled drinks, nightly mopping or hose-downs and heavy crowds.

  • Satin with anti-slip treatment at entrances, bars and service stations.
  • Kitchens are better in urethane cement, which handles hot water, grease and thermal shock. Why
  • Acidic spills (cocktails, citrus, wine) can dull the guard over time; a periodic re-guard and burnish restores it.
  • Schedule around your hours. For late-night venues, work usually happens in the morning and daytime; for daytime restaurants, overnight.

Breweries and taprooms

Wynwood is known for its breweries, and a brewery has two very different floors under one roof:

  • Taproom and seating: Usual floor: Polished concrete, satin, anti-slip; Why: Durable, easy to mop, fits the look
  • Brewhouse and cellar: Usual floor: Urethane cement, sloped to drains; Why: Constant water, hot caustic and acid cleaning, and spilled beer, which is acidic
  • Cold rooms: Usual floor: Coating or polished, planned around temperature; Why: See cold storage
  • Storage and packaging: Usual floor: Polished concrete, matte; Why: Dust-free, forklift-friendly

Retail, showrooms and the Design District

Boutiques and showrooms in Wynwood, and the luxury stores and design showrooms in the neighboring Design District, usually want a more refined floor:

  • Semi-gloss to high gloss to reflect lighting, with satin at entrances where rain comes in.
  • Salt and pepper or exposed aggregate for a more finished look.
  • Overnight work so stores open as normal.

Lofts, creative offices and new buildings

Wynwood is moving from an industrial district to a mixed-use neighborhood, with new mid-rise apartments, offices and hotels going up among the warehouses.

  • Lofts in converted buildings on a ground-level slab are usually straightforward to polish.
  • Condos with a unit below fall under Florida's sound rules, which usually rule out a bare polished slab. Sound rules · Microcement
  • New construction: plan the polish before the pour, and protect the slab during construction. Specifying polished concrete
  • Creative offices: satin salt-and-pepper, done over weekends.

Timing and permits

  • Miami Art Week in early December is the busiest time of year for Wynwood galleries and venues. Schedule floor work well before it, typically late summer or early fall.
  • Openings and events: work backwards from your next opening, allowing time for the guard to cure before crowds.
  • Permits: Wynwood is in the City of Miami, so building matters go through the City of Miami. Tenants need landlord approval.
  • Access: street parking and loading are tight, so plan equipment delivery and waste removal with the contractor.

Common questions

Can an old Wynwood warehouse floor be polished?

Usually. Old garment and factory slabs often polish well, but expect oil stains, bolt holes from machinery, trenches and patches. Many owners keep that industrial character; a test panel shows how your slab will look.

What finish works best for an art gallery?

Usually a matte or satin finish. High gloss reflects gallery lighting and can compete with the work on the walls. A cream or salt-and-pepper look keeps the floor neutral.

Can you polish a bar or restaurant floor without closing?

Yes. For late-night venues, work usually happens in the morning and daytime when you're closed; for daytime restaurants, overnight. Phasing keeps part of the space open if needed.

Is polished concrete right for a brewery?

For the taproom, yes, with a satin finish and anti-slip treatment. The brewhouse and cellar get constant water, hot caustic and acid cleaning, and spilled beer, so they're usually better with urethane cement sloped to drains.

When should galleries schedule floor work?

Avoid the run-up to Miami Art Week in early December and your own opening dates. Late summer and early fall are usually easiest, and the floor should be finished before artwork is reinstalled.

Can I polish the floor in my Wynwood loft or condo?

In a converted building on a ground-level slab, often yes. In a condo with a unit below, Florida's sound rules usually rule out a bare polished slab.

Who handles permits in Wynwood?

Wynwood is in the City of Miami, so building matters go through the City of Miami. Tenants also need landlord approval.
