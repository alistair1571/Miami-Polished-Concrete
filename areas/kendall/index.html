1970s Kendall homes: check before grinding

Kendall grew fast. Its population roughly doubled between 1970 and 1980, so a large share of homes date from that decade, usually concrete block on slabs, later covered with vinyl, tile or carpet. They're often good candidates for polishing, with three checks first:

  • Asbestos in old adhesive. Black mastic under vinyl tile or old ceramic from before the mid-1980s often contains asbestos. Have the tile and adhesive lab-tested separately before anyone grinds. What to do
  • Terrazzo. Some older homes have terrazzo under the carpet. Restore it rather than grinding it like concrete. Terrazzo
  • Five decades of patches. Plumbing re-pipes, additions and repairs leave patches that show once polished. A test patch shows you how your slab will look.
Photo: Polished floor in a 1970s Kendall house after tile removal

After the tile comes up

  • Thinset shadows and tack-strip holes are ground off or filled; some faint marks may remain.
  • Your floor gets lower by roughly the thickness of the tile and thinset, so baseboards, door clearances and transitions may need attention.
  • Tile demolition is the dusty part. Make sure it's contained with zip walls and extraction.
  • Satin salt-and-pepper is the most forgiving finish on a reclaimed slab.

HOAs, cluster homes and condos

Much of Kendall is organized into communities: cluster homes, townhomes and garden-style condos, many with HOAs or condo associations.

  • HOAs may set work hours, require contractors to be registered, or need notice before noisy work. Check before you book.
  • Townhomes and cluster homes on their own slab are usually straightforward to polish.
  • Garden and mid-rise condos: in a unit with another unit below, Florida's sound rules (IIC 50 and STC 50) usually rule out a bare polished slab. Ground-floor units are exempt from that rule. Sound rules · Microcement for upper floors

Kids, pets and family homes

  • Satin rather than high gloss for better grip and fewer visible footprints.
  • Rugs where kids play and dogs run and turn.
  • Spills wipe up easily; acidic ones (juice, soda, citrus) should be wiped promptly.
  • Claws: densified concrete handles them far better than wood or vinyl plank.
  • Cool floor: it stays cool underfoot through a Kendall summer.

Flooding and storms

Parts of Kendall sit in FEMA shallow-flooding zones, where heavy rain can pond around homes. Polished concrete isn't ruined by water the way wood, laminate and vinyl plank often are: after a flood it's cleaned, dried and, if needed, re-burnished. Keep rugs you can lift and dry.

Permits in Kendall

Kendall isn't a city. It's an unincorporated area governed by Miami-Dade County, so building matters go through the county rather than a city building department. Interior polishing on a house often doesn't need much, but your contractor should confirm what applies, especially for condos, additions or anything structural.

Nearby Pinecrest and Palmetto Bay are separate villages with their own rules.

Dadeland and Kendall businesses

Dadeland is Kendall's business district, with offices, shops, restaurants and medical practices along Kendall Drive, US-1 and the Palmetto.

  • Offices: satin salt-and-pepper, done over weekends.
  • Medical and dental offices: satin, easy to clean; ask about sealing joints.
  • Restaurants and retail: satin with anti-slip at entrances, or semi-gloss for showrooms.
  • Hangars and aviation businesses near Kendall-Tamiami Executive Airport often need chemical-resistant coatings in work areas. Coatings

Common questions

How much does polished concrete cost in Kendall?

A typical Kendall home with a satin finish after tile removal commonly runs $6 to $9 per sq ft, plus $2 to $7 for tile removal. A 1,200 sq ft ground floor with several rooms usually comes to roughly $7,500 to $12,000 for the polishing.

My Kendall house was built in the 1970s. What should I check?

Test any black adhesive under old vinyl or tile for asbestos before grinding, check for terrazzo under carpet, and expect patched areas from decades of plumbing work to show once polished.

Who handles permits in Kendall?

Kendall is unincorporated, so building matters go through Miami-Dade County rather than a city. Your HOA may also have its own rules for work hours and contractors.

Can I polish the floor in my Kendall condo?

In a unit with another unit below, usually not as a bare polished slab, because of Florida's sound rules for floors between units. Ground-floor units are exempt from that rule; your association must still approve.

Is polished concrete a good choice if my street floods?

Yes. Polished concrete isn't ruined by water the way wood, laminate and vinyl plank often are. After flooding it's cleaned, dried and re-burnished if needed.

Is polished concrete good for kids and dogs?

It's durable and easy to clean. Choose satin rather than high gloss for grip, add rugs where kids play and dogs run, and use a pH-neutral cleaner.

Which parts of Kendall do you cover?

All of Kendall, including Dadeland, Kendall West, The Hammocks, Kendale Lakes, The Crossings and Three Lakes, plus nearby Pinecrest and Palmetto Bay.
