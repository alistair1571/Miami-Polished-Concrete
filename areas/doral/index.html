Doral warehouse floors

The Airport West and Doral area is one of the tightest industrial markets in South Florida, built around Miami International Airport's cargo terminals and the road links to PortMiami. Most buildings are tilt-wall shells on large, bare slabs that shed dust under forklift and pallet-jack traffic.

Polishing is usually the most cost-effective floor upgrade: it hardens the surface, stops the dust getting into stock and equipment, and cuts cleaning to a scrubber pass. Most Doral warehouse floors are finished at a matte or satin sheen with a cream look, which keeps the grind shallow and the cost low.

What a typical Doral warehouse job includes

  • Removing old paint, striping or failed epoxy in forklift lanes.
  • Joint filling with semi-rigid polyurea so joint edges stop breaking down under wheels.
  • Working around racking: aisles and open areas first; fixed racking is usually left in place.
  • Night and weekend shifts so receiving and dispatch keep running.
  • Re-striping aisles and safety markings at handover.

More detail: commercial polished concrete. For refrigerated and perishables buildings near the airport, see cold storage floors.

Small-bay units, landlords and tenant turnover

Much of Doral's industrial space is small-bay and flex units, and space under 10,000 sq ft stays in short supply. These units change hands regularly, which makes the gap between tenants the natural time to redo the floor.

  • For landlords and property managers: with Miami-Dade industrial vacancy rising in 2026, older units compete harder for tenants. A clean, polished floor is one of the first things a prospective tenant sees on a tour, and it's far cheaper than most other upgrades.
  • Between tenants: the unit is empty, there's no racking or stock to work around, and the job can be finished before the new lease starts.
  • For tenants: get landlord consent in writing, and check your lease for restoration clauses. Polishing improves the landlord's own slab, so approval is usually straightforward.
  • Several units at once: doing multiple bays in one visit spreads setup costs and brings the per-foot price down.

Aviation parts, MRO and import/export businesses

Airport West and Doral are home to a large number of aviation parts, maintenance and import/export companies that want to be close to the airport. Their floors often mix very different uses under one roof, so the right answer is usually a split specification:

  • Parts storage, aisles, shipping: Usual choice: Polished concrete, matte or satin; Why: Dust-free, hard-wearing, low maintenance
  • Offices and customer areas: Usual choice: Polished concrete, satin; Why: Clean, modern look at low cost
  • Work cells handling hydraulic fluids, fuels or solvents: Usual choice: Chemical-resistant coating; Why: Polished concrete can stain if these sit on it
  • Battery charging and wash-down areas: Usual choice: Chemical-resistant coating; Why: Acid and water resistance

A contractor who offers both, or tells you where each belongs, is more useful than one who polishes everything. Epoxy and coatings

Loading docks, afternoon storms and slabs that move

Wet dock doors

Doral's summer downpours blow in through open dock doors, and that's where people and pallet jacks slip. Keep the first bays inside each door at a matte finish, add matting, and consider an anti-slip treatment there even if the rest of the floor is satin.

Panels that rock under trucks

If slab panels move or "pump" when loaded trucks or forklifts cross the joints, polishing won't fix it. The slab needs stabilizing and the joints repaired first. Filling joints over a moving slab just means the filler fails. A good survey should check for this before quoting a polish.

Hurricane season

A polished slab isn't damaged by standing water the way coatings and tile can be. After a storm it's cleaned and, if needed, re-burnished.

Showrooms, retail, restaurants and offices

Doral's showrooms and trade counters, the shops and restaurants around Downtown Doral, and the office parks along NW 87th Avenue all suit polished concrete. Showrooms usually go to a semi-gloss or high gloss to reflect the lighting; restaurants and offices usually stay at satin, which hides scuffs and is safer when floors get wet. Chain retail has used low-gloss polished floors in Doral for years.

Work can be done overnight so you open as normal the next day.

Doral homes and townhomes

Doral became a city in 2003, and much of its housing is relatively new: single-family homes, townhomes and mid-rise condos, most built on slabs and finished with tile. More is on the way, including a proposal for about 900 homes at the Miami International Mall site.

  • Newer slabs are usually sound and flat, so they polish well once the tile is removed.
  • Older adhesive is less likely in homes built after the mid-1980s, but if you find black mastic in an older property, test it before grinding.
  • HOA and condo rules can limit working hours, and in condos with a unit below, Florida's sound rules usually rule out a bare polished slab. See the condo guide.
  • New builds: if you're building or remodeling, polish before cabinets and baseboards go in.

Common questions

How much does warehouse floor polishing cost in Doral?

Large Doral warehouse floors usually price at the low end of Miami's $3 to $8 per sq ft range for a matte or satin finish. Small-bay units cost more per foot. Coating removal, joint filling and repairs are itemized separately.

Can you polish our warehouse while we keep shipping?

Yes. Work is phased by bay or aisle, or done at nights and weekends, so receiving and dispatch keep running.

Our slab panels move when trucks cross the joints. Will polishing fix that?

No. Panels that rock or pump under load need stabilizing and joint repair first. Polishing and joint filling come after the slab is solid.

We're between tenants. Is that a good time to polish?

It's the best time. The floor is empty, there's no racking to work around, and a polished floor helps the unit show well to the next tenant.

Is polished concrete suitable for an aviation parts or maintenance shop?

For storage, aisles and offices, yes. Work areas where hydraulic fluids and solvents are handled are usually better protected with a chemical-resistant coating.

Do you polish floors in Doral homes and townhomes?

Yes. Many Doral homes are newer builds with tile over a sound slab, which usually polishes well once the tile is removed.

Which parts of Doral do you cover?

All of Doral, including the NW 25th Street and NW 87th Avenue corridors, the Doral Business Park area, Downtown Doral and the residential neighborhoods, plus Medley, Miami Springs and the airport area.
