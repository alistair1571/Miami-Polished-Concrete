How we document projects

Most project galleries show a few glossy after shots. We'd rather show you what you'd actually get, including the patches and character of a real Miami slab.

  • Same angle, same light. Before and after photos are taken from the same spot, in similar light, without heavy editing.
  • What was underneath. Every project notes what was found once the old floor came up: adhesive, patches, terrazzo, moisture.
  • The exact finish. Aggregate exposure, sheen and final grit, so you can ask for the same result.
  • Time and constraints. How long it took, and what had to be worked around: tenants, residents, opening hours.
  • Cost range. A realistic range for the job, so you can compare with the cost guide.
  • Permission first. Projects are published with the customer's consent; homes are identified by neighborhood only.
