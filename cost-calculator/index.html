      How the estimate works

      Each finish has a typical Miami-Dade price range per square foot. The calculator multiplies that by your area, adds any extras you tick, then adjusts for size, the slab's condition and access. Small jobs cost more per square foot because equipment and setup cost the same either way, and very large floors cost less.

      What's not included

      Your written quote will list these separately if your project needs them:

      • Structural repairs, or lifting sunken or root-damaged slabs
      • Asbestos testing results and any abatement
      • Major moisture remediation beyond a moisture-blocking primer
      • Moving furniture, new baseboards and door trimming
      • Permits, association fees and engineer's reports

      Questions about the calculator

      How accurate is the calculator?

      It gives a realistic range based on typical Miami-Dade prices for the finish, size and extras you choose. The final price depends on your slab's condition, moisture and access, which a contractor confirms at a free site visit.

      Why is the price per square foot higher for small jobs?

      Equipment, setup and travel cost the same whether the floor is 200 or 2,000 square feet, so small jobs cost more per square foot and usually carry a minimum charge.

      What isn't included in the estimate?

      Structural repairs, asbestos abatement, moving furniture, baseboard replacement, permits and major moisture remediation. Your written quote will list them if needed.

      Do condo and high-rise projects cost more?

      Usually a little. Restricted working hours, elevator bookings, protecting common areas and moving debris down the building add labor.

      How do I get an exact price?

      Send your estimate with the quote form. A licensed Miami-Dade contractor will visit, test the slab and give you a written, fixed quote.
