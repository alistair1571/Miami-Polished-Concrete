Not sure which you need?

  • Sound slab, want the most durable, lowest-maintenance floor: Polished concrete
  • Upper-floor condo with a unit below: Microcement over a sound-rated build-up
  • Keeping existing tile, or walls and showers: Microcement
  • Chemicals, wash-downs or bright zone colors: Epoxy or urethane cement
  • Terrazzo under the carpet: Terrazzo restoration
  • Existing polished floor gone dull: Maintenance and restoration
  • Pool deck, patio or outdoor slab: Pool deck resurfacing
