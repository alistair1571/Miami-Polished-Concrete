We're a lead generation website. How it works

What a lead generation website is

Lead generation sites are common in home services, but they don't always say so. Some look like a local contractor, with a local-sounding name, stock photos and claims about "our crew" and "our years in business", when the work is actually done by whichever company buys the enquiry.

We'd rather be clear. Miami Polished Concrete doesn't employ installers or own equipment. What we do is:

  • Publish guides on costs, finishes, condo rules, older homes and commercial floors in Miami, with the technical details checked by the contractor we work with.
  • Take your enquiry through our form or phone line.
  • Pass it to one contractor, with your consent, who contacts you to arrange an assessment and quote.

From that point on, your relationship is with the contractor. They quote, contract, schedule, carry out and guarantee the work.

How we choose a contractor

  • Active Florida licence and any local certificates the work requires, checked on the DBPR and county records.
  • Current general liability insurance and, where required, workers' compensation.
  • Photos and addresses of completed polished concrete jobs, and references.
  • Ongoing feedback from customers we've sent. Contractors who don't follow through stop receiving enquiries.

Check for yourself

Don't rely on us alone. Before you sign anything:

  • Look up the contractor's licence on the Florida DBPR licence search.
  • Ask for a certificate of insurance, and for a condo, one naming your association.
  • Read their Google reviews, not just the ones on this site.
  • Get the quote and warranty in writing.

What happens to your details

  • One contractor only. When you submit the form or call, your details go to the one contractor handling your area, and only because you've agreed to it.
  • Never sold. We don't sell or rent your details or add you to marketing lists.
  • Calls may be recorded and tracked so we can see which enquiries come from this site. That's how the contractor knows what to pay us for.
  • Delete on request. Email us and we'll remove your details from our records.

Common questions

Are you a polished concrete contractor?

No. We're a lead generation website. We publish guides and pass enquiries to one independent, licensed contractor, who does all the work.

How do you make money?

The contractor pays us for the enquiries we send them. You never pay us anything, and we don't add a fee or markup to the contractor's price.

Will I get calls from lots of companies?

No. Your details go to one contractor only. We don't sell them or send them to other companies.

Is your advice biased?

We have an interest: we're paid when people ask for quotes. We try to earn trust by being straight, including telling people when polished concrete isn't right for them, such as in most upper-floor condos.

What if I'm not happy with the contractor?

Raise it with the contractor first, since your contract is with them. Then tell us. We can't arbitrate or give refunds, but we take complaints seriously and stop sending enquiries to contractors who don't treat customers properly.

Can I contact the contractor directly instead?

Yes. Once you know who they are, you're free to deal with them directly. Nothing about using this site ties you to anything.

How do I check the contractor is licensed?

Ask for their licence number and look it up on the Florida DBPR licence search, and ask for a certificate of insurance. We encourage everyone to do this.
